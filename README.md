# [Tasker](https://tasker.4nirudh.org)

A to-do list you just talk to. Type tasks the way you'd say them, like "gym next monday 7am legs, blue" or "groceries in 2 hours yellow, milk eggs chicken rice", and Tasker works out the date, time, color and details for you.

- Every task gets a reminder 3 hours before it's due. Add your own with "remind me 30 min before", "remind me in 2 hours" or "remind me at 6pm".
- Tasks are sorted by due date, color coded, and synced across devices.
- Calendar view, multiple themes, and a mobile web app.
